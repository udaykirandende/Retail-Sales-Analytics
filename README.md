🚀 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard | SQL • Power BI • DAX

📌 Project Overview

Designed and developed an end-to-end Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard using SQL and Power BI to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into actionable business insights. The project analyzes customer behavior, revenue performance, seller contribution, payment preferences, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction across Brazil.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify high-value customers and sellers, evaluate market trends, and optimize customer experience through data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Problem

E-commerce businesses generate large volumes of transactional data but often struggle to extract meaningful insights regarding:

Revenue growth and sales performance
Customer purchasing behavior
Seller performance and market contribution
Delivery efficiency across regions
Customer satisfaction and review trends

This project addresses these challenges through an interactive multi-page analytical dashboard.

📈 Key Findings

✅ São Paulo (SP) is the largest revenue-generating state, contributing approximately 42% of total revenue.

✅ Credit Card transactions account for the majority of purchases, making it the dominant payment method.

✅ Revenue is concentrated among a small percentage of top-performing sellers.

✅ Monthly revenue demonstrated a strong upward trend throughout 2017 and early 2018, indicating sustained business growth and increasing customer demand.

✅ Revenue peaked during late 2017 to early 2018, highlighting periods of highest sales activity.

✅ The sharp decline in the final months is attributable to the dataset ending period rather than an actual

✅ Customer satisfaction remains strong, with an average review score above 4/5.

✅ More than 97% of orders are successfully delivered, indicating efficient fulfillment operations.

✅ Delivery performance varies across regions, with remote states experiencing longer delivery times.
